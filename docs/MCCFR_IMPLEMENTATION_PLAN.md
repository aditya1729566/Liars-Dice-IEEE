## Architecture and estimator

Create an isolated C++20 trainer with deterministic `mt19937_64`, checkpointed regrets, average-strategy sums and RNG state. For each iteration and each traverser, independently sample fair dice and starter; sample opponent actions from current regret matching; enumerate all abstract-game actions at traverser nodes. At a traverser node, add each `u(a) - Σ_b σ(b)u(b)` to cumulative regret. There is no extra opponent-reach multiplier: the probability of visiting that node already samples chance and opponent reach, which cancels the external-sampling importance denominator. At opponent nodes, add the current strategy to the average sum. Prior sampled actions of that opponent provide the implicit own-reach weighting, as in the simple averaging variant of external-sampling MCCFR. Positive cumulative regrets determine the current strategy; cumulative regrets themselves are never truncated.

The toy verification game has one die per player, all legal opening bids and quantity-two raises, full public history, and exact best-response evaluation. The five-dice model optimizes a **single round's +1/−1 point utility**, not first-to-five match victory. It samples actual five-die chance outcomes and resolves calls with actual five-die rules. To keep memory bounded, it restricts bids to opening quantity one on each face or quantity two on the private modal face, then raises by one or two using the current face or private modal face. It keys on private dice histogram, starter, player, and the last two public bids. The last-two-bid key has imperfect recall, so full-game equilibrium and theoretical MCCFR convergence guarantees do not apply to this abstraction. At inference, engine legality remains authoritative, and unseen or off-abstraction states use a documented fallback.

Use a versioned `mccfr_external_v1` policy and separate checkpoint files. Add agent allowlists, checksum validation, frontend card, research explanation, replay label, and tournament policy hashing. Keep `cfr2` as default and do not alter existing policy files or historical evidence. Train staged checkpoints under the existing two-worker, 1.5 GiB artifact, and 2 GiB free-disk guards. Evaluate held-out paired seeds and report only completed, measured results.
